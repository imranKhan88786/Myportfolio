A Decade of Quality Excellence

With over a decade in software testing, I have carved out a reputation for ensuring the quality, performance, and security of diverse applications, from web and mobile to desktop. This extends to web and API automation as well as cutting-edge areas like NFC, RFID, and IoT devices.

One of my career milestones was leading the QA engineering team at Swiggy, India's top online food delivery platform. I initiated and rolled out frameworks for web, API, UI, and app automation testing, leveraging tools like Cypress, JMeter, NewRelic, and Git.

Beyond automation, I showcased versatility with manual testing, diligently documenting test plans, results, and bug reports while championing Agile methodologies via JIRA. My role included vendor management and cross-POD projects, instituting documentation processes pivotal in elevating product quality.
